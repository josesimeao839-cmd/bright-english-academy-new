📚 Grammar — Likes, Dislikes & Food

❤️ I like...

Use I like to talk about food or drinks you enjoy.

I like + food/drink.
I like rice.
I like juice.
I like bananas.

❌ I don't like...

Use I don't like to say that you do not enjoy something.

I don't like + food/drink.
I don't like fish.
I don't like coffee.

❓ Do you like...?

Do you like + food/drink?
Do you like chicken?
Yes, I do.
No, I don't.

⭐ Favorite Food

My favorite food is + food.
My favorite food is rice.
My favorite drink is juice.

🍽️ Ordering Food

I would like + food/drink, please.
I would like chicken and rice, please.
I would like some water, please.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Marta and Kelvin are having lunch.

Marta: Hi, Kelvin! Are you hungry?
Kelvin: Yes, I am very hungry.
Marta: What would you like to eat?
Kelvin: I would like chicken and rice, please.
Marta: Good choice! Do you like vegetables?
Kelvin: Yes, I like vegetables.
Marta: Would you like some juice?
Kelvin: Yes, please. I would like orange juice.
Marta: Do you like coffee?
Kelvin: No, I don't. I prefer tea.
Marta: What is your favorite food?
Kelvin: My favorite food is chicken and rice.
Marta: That sounds delicious!
Kelvin: Thank you!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your favorite food and drinks.

Try to use:

  • I like...
  • I don't like...
  • My favorite food is...
  • My favorite drink is...
  • I would like...

Example:

My favorite food is chicken and rice. I like bananas and apples. I like orange juice. I don't like fish. My favorite drink is juice. For breakfast, I eat bread and eggs. I would like some water, please.
