⭐ Important Expressions

What time is it?
Que horas são?

It's three o'clock.

It's quarter past five.
São cinco e quinze.

It's quarter to six.
São quinze para as seis.

📚 Grammar — Time Expressions

1️⃣ Asking the Time

What time is it?
A: What time is it?
B: It's seven o'clock.

2️⃣ At + Time

Use at before a specific time.

at + time
I wake up at 6:00.

I go to school at 7:00.

I have dinner at 7:30.

3️⃣ In the Morning / Afternoon / Evening

in the morning
in the afternoon
in the evening
I study English in the morning.

I play football in the afternoon.

I watch television in the evening.

4️⃣ At Night

at night
I sleep at night.

I read a book at night.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Inocêncio and Antônio are talking about their daily schedules.

Inocêncio: Hi, Antônio! What time do you wake up?
Antônio: I wake up at six o'clock. What about you?
Inocêncio: I wake up at half past six.
Antônio: What time do you go to school?
Inocêncio: I go to school at seven thirty.
Antônio: That's early!
Inocêncio: Yes! What time do your classes start?
Antônio: My classes start at eight o'clock.
Inocêncio: What time do you have lunch?
Antônio: I have lunch at twelve thirty.
Inocêncio: And when do you study English?
Antônio: I study English in the afternoon.
Inocêncio: That's great! I study English in the evening.
Antônio: What time do you go to bed?
Inocêncio: I go to bed at ten o'clock.
Antônio: Me too. See you tomorrow!
Inocêncio: See you tomorrow!
