💬 Classroom Expressions

1️⃣ Asking for Help

Can you help me, please?
Pode ajudar-me, por favor?

2️⃣ Asking for Repetition

Can you repeat, please?
Pode repetir, por favor?

3️⃣ Asking for Permission

Can I come in?
Posso entrar?

4️⃣ Saying You Don't Understand

I don't understand.
Eu não entendo.

5️⃣ Asking How to Say Something

How do you say this in English?
Como se diz isto em inglês?

📚 Grammar — Can I...? / Can you...?

1️⃣ Can I...?

Use Can I...? when asking for permission.

Can I come in?
Posso entrar?

Can I ask a question?
Posso fazer uma pergunta?

2️⃣ Can you...?

Use Can you...? when asking another person to do something.

Can you repeat, please?
Pode repetir, por favor?

Can you help me?
Pode ajudar-me?

3️⃣ Simple Answers

Yes, you can.
Sim, podes.

No, you can't.
Não, não podes.

💬 Real-life Conversation

David is in an English class.

Teacher: Good morning, David.

David: Good morning, teacher.

Teacher: Please open your book.

David: Sorry, I don't understand.

Teacher: Open your book, please.

David: Oh, okay. Thank you.

David: Can I ask a question?

Teacher: Yes, of course.

David: How do you say "caderno" in English?

Teacher: We say "notebook."

David: Notebook. Thank you!

Teacher: You're welcome.

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Classroom English

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to say:

  • Good morning, teacher.
  • Can I ask a question?
  • I don't understand.
  • Can you repeat, please?
  • Can you help me?
  • Thank you.

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 classroom sentences.

Try to use:

  • Can I...?
  • Can you...?
  • I don't understand.
  • Thank you.
Example:

Good morning, teacher. Can I ask a question? I don't understand. Can you repeat, please? Can you help me? Thank you.
