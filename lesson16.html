📍 There Is

Use there is for one thing.

THERE IS + singular noun
There is a sofa in the living room.
Há um sofá na sala.
There is a bed in my bedroom.
Há uma cama no meu quarto.
There is a television in the room.
Há uma televisão no quarto.

📍 There Are

Use there are for two or more things.

THERE ARE + plural noun
There are two chairs in the kitchen.
Há duas cadeiras na cozinha.
There are three windows in the house.
Há três janelas na casa.
There are four rooms upstairs.
Há quatro quartos no andar de cima.

❌ Negative Forms

Use there isn't for one thing and there aren't for two or more.

There isn't + singular noun
There aren't + plural noun
There isn't a television in my bedroom.
Não há televisão no meu quarto.
There aren't any chairs in the garage.
Não há cadeiras na garagem.

❓ Questions with There Is / There Are

Is there + a/an + singular noun?
Are there + plural noun?
Is there a bathroom upstairs?
Há uma casa de banho no andar de cima?

Yes, there is.
Are there two bedrooms?
Há dois quartos?

Yes, there are.
Is there a garden?
Há um jardim?

No, there isn't.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Vania and Jafar are talking about Jafar's house.

Vania: Hi, Jafar! Tell me about your house.
Jafar: Sure! My house is small but comfortable.
Vania: How many rooms are there?
Jafar: There are four rooms.
Vania: Is there a living room?
Jafar: Yes, there is. There is a big sofa and a television.
Vania: What about the kitchen?
Jafar: There is a table and four chairs in the kitchen.
Vania: Are there two bedrooms?
Jafar: Yes, there are. My bedroom is next to the bathroom.
Vania: Is there a garden?
Jafar: Yes, there is a small garden behind the house.
Vania: Your house sounds nice!
Jafar: Thank you, Vania!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences describing your home or an imaginary home.

Try to use:

  • There is...
  • There are...
  • There isn't...
  • There aren't...
  • Is there...?
  • Are there...?

Example:

There are five rooms in my house. There is a living room. There is a kitchen. There are two bedrooms. There is a bathroom. There are two chairs in the kitchen. There isn't a garage.
