🕐 Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for routines, habits and facts.

I / You / We / They + verb

I study English.
They work every day.

He / She / It + verb + s

She studies English.
He works every day.

❌ Negative

I / You / We / They + don't + verb
He / She / It + doesn't + verb
I don't work on Sunday.
She doesn't drink coffee.

❓ Questions

Do + I / you / we / they + verb?
Does + he / she / it + verb?
Do you study English?
Does she work here?

⏮️ Past Simple

Use the Past Simple for completed actions in the past.

Regular verbs: work → worked play → played watch → watched

Irregular verbs: go → went eat → ate see → saw have → had
I watched a movie yesterday.
She visited her friend last week.
They went to school yesterday.
He ate rice for lunch.

❌ Negative

Subject + didn't + base verb

I didn't go.
She didn't eat.

❓ Questions

Did + subject + base verb?

Did you go to school?
Did she eat breakfast?

🔮 Future — Will / Going To

Will

Use will for predictions, promises and decisions made at the moment.

Subject + will + verb

I will help you.
It will rain tomorrow.

Going to

Use be going to for plans and intentions.

Subject + am/is/are + going to + verb

I am going to study tonight.
She is going to travel tomorrow.

💬 Real-life Conversation

José and Álvaro are reviewing their English course.

José: Hi, Álvaro! How are you?
Álvaro: I'm fine, thank you. And you?
José: I'm great. Are you ready for the final review?
Álvaro: Yes! I have studied a lot.
José: What do you usually do in the morning?
Álvaro: I usually wake up at six o'clock and study English.
José: What did you do yesterday?
Álvaro: I went to school and studied English.
José: What are you going to do tomorrow?
Álvaro: I'm going to study again.
José: That's great! What will you do after the course?
Álvaro: I will continue practicing English.
José: Excellent! Keep practicing every day.
Álvaro: Thank you! I will.

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 8–10 sentences about yourself.

Try to include:

  • Present Simple
  • Past Simple
  • Future
  • One adverb of frequency
  • Two prepositions
  • Daily routine vocabulary

Example:

I usually wake up early. I study English every day. Yesterday, I went to school. I studied English with my friends. Tomorrow, I am going to study again. I will continue learning English. My books are on my table. My school is next to my house. I always try to improve my English.
