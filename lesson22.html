📚 Grammar — Talking About Weather

1️⃣ Use "It is" / "It's"

In English, we normally use it when talking about the weather.

It is + weather adjective.
It's sunny.
Está ensolarado.

2️⃣ Weather + today

It is + adjective + today.
It's hot today.
Hoje está quente.

3️⃣ Ask about the weather

What's the weather like?

= Como está o tempo?

A: What's the weather like?
B: It's sunny and warm.

4️⃣ Talking about rain

It is raining.

It is not raining.
It isn't raining today.
Hoje não está a chover.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Lúcia and Marta are talking before class.

Lúcia: Good morning, Marta!
Marta: Good morning, Lúcia!
Lúcia: What's the weather like today?
Marta: It's sunny and warm.
Lúcia: That's nice! Is it windy?
Marta: No, it isn't. It's very calm.
Lúcia: Do you like sunny days?
Marta: Yes, I do. I love sunny days.
Lúcia: What about rainy days?
Marta: I like rainy days too, but I don't like storms.
Lúcia: Which season do you like?
Marta: I like spring because the weather is warm.
Lúcia: I like summer because I love sunny weather.
Marta: That sounds great!
Lúcia: Yes! Let's go to class.
Marta: Let's go!

  • It's raining...
