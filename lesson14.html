📚 There is / There are in Town

There is + singular place
There are + plural places
There is a hospital in my town.
Há um hospital na minha cidade.
There are three schools in my area.
Há três escolas na minha área.

📍 Prepositions of Place

These words help us describe the location of places.

English Portuguese Example
near perto de The school is near the park.
next to ao lado de The bank is next to the supermarket.
opposite em frente de The bank is opposite the school.
between entre The pharmacy is between the bank and the market.
behind atrás de The park is behind the school.
in front of em frente de The bus stop is in front of the hospital.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Asking about places in town.

James: Excuse me. Is there a bank near here?
Maria: Yes, there is.
James: Where is it?
Maria: It is next to the supermarket.
James: Is there a pharmacy near the bank?
Maria: Yes. The pharmacy is opposite the bank.
James: Thank you very much!
Maria: You're welcome!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your town or neighborhood.

Try to use:

  • There is
  • There are
  • near
  • next to
  • opposite
  • between

Example:

There is a hospital in my town. There are two schools. There is a supermarket near my house. The bank is next to the supermarket. The pharmacy is opposite the bank. There is a park between the school and the hospital.
