🔢 Countable Nouns

Countable nouns are things we can count: one, two, three...

one apple → two apples → three apples
one egg → two eggs → three eggs
I have one apple.
Eu tenho uma maçã.
I have three apples.
Eu tenho três maçãs.

🥛 Uncountable Nouns

Some foods and drinks are normally treated as substances rather than individual objects.

water ✓
milk ✓
rice ✓
bread ✓
sugar ✓
❌ I have three waters.
✅ I have some water.
❌ I need two rices.
✅ I need some rice.

➕ Some & Any

SOME → usually affirmative sentences
ANY → usually questions and negative sentences
I have some apples.
Eu tenho algumas maçãs.
We have some milk.
Nós temos leite.
Do you have any eggs?
Tens ovos?
I don't have any bread.
Eu não tenho pão.

❓ Much & Many

MANY + countable nouns
MUCH + uncountable nouns
How many apples do you have?
Quantas maçãs tens?
How much water do you drink?
Quanta água bebes?
I don't have many eggs.
Eu não tenho muitos ovos.
I don't drink much milk.
Eu não bebo muito leite.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Vania and Jafar are talking about food and drinks.

Vania: Hi, Jafar! What are you having for breakfast?
Jafar: Hi, Vania! I'm having some bread and two eggs.
Vania: Do you have any fruit?
Jafar: Yes, I have some bananas and an apple.
Vania: How much water do you drink every day?
Jafar: I drink a lot of water every day.
Vania: Do you like milk?
Jafar: Yes, I do. I drink some milk in the morning.
Vania: What food do you like most?
Jafar: I really like chicken and rice.
Vania: Sounds delicious!
Jafar: Thank you!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your favorite food and drinks.

Try to use:

  • I like...
  • I don't like...
  • some
  • any
  • much
  • many

Example:

I like chicken and rice. I also like apples. I usually drink some milk in the morning. I drink a lot of water every day. I don't drink much soda. I don't eat many sweets.
