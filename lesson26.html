🚌 Transportation Vocabulary

Learn the most useful transportation words.

🚌 Bus

Bus — Autocarro

Example: I go to school by bus.

🚕 Taxi

Taxi — Táxi

Example: I take a taxi to the airport.

🚗 Car

Car — Carro

Train — Comboio

Useful Questions

Where is the airport?

How can I get to the hotel?

Useful Answers

Go straight.

Turn left.

Turn right.

It is next to the bank.

It is opposite the school.

It is near the hotel.

It is far from here.

🧠 Where / How / When

📍 WHERE

Use where to ask about a place.

Where is the airport?

Where is the hotel?

❓ HOW

Use how to ask about method or way.

How do I get there?

How can I travel there?

⏰ WHEN

Use when to ask about time.

When does the bus leave?

When does the train arrive?

📍 Prepositions of Place

next to = ao lado de
near = perto de
far from = longe de
opposite = em frente de
between = entre
behind = atrás de
in front of = em frente de

💬 Real-life Conversation

Marta and Samuel are at a bus station.

Marta: Excuse me. Where is the bus station?
Samuel: It is near the market.
Marta: How can I get there?
Samuel: Go straight and turn left.
Marta: Is it far from here?
Samuel: No, it is very near.
Marta: Thank you very much.
Samuel: You're welcome.
Marta: When does the bus leave?
Samuel: It leaves at nine o'clock.
Marta: Great. I need one ticket, please.
Samuel: Sure. Here you are.
Marta: Thank you!
Samuel: Have a nice trip!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk about a trip.

Speak for 1–2 minutes.


  • Where are you going?
  • How will you travel?
  • Who will travel with you?
  • Where will you stay?
  • What places will you visit?
  • When will you leave?

  • Hotel
