👪 People in a Family

Here are some common family relationships.

👨 Father

Your male parent.

This is my father.
Este é o meu pai.

👩 Mother

Your female parent.

This is my mother.
Esta é a minha mãe.

👦 Brother

I have one brother.
Eu tenho um irmão.

👧 Sister

I have two sisters.
Eu tenho duas irmãs.

📚 Grammar — Possessive Adjectives

1️⃣ My

Use my to talk about something that belongs to you.

This is my mother.
Esta é a minha mãe.

My father is a teacher.
O meu pai é professor.

2️⃣ Your

Use your when something belongs to another person.

Is this your brother?
Este é o teu irmão?

3️⃣ His

Use his for a male person.

His mother is a teacher.
A mãe dele é professora.

4️⃣ Her

Use her for a female person.

Her father is a doctor.
O pai dela é médico.

5️⃣ Have / Has

I have two brothers.
Eu tenho dois irmãos.

She has one sister.
Ela tem uma irmã.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Maria and Daniel are talking about their families.

Maria: Hi, Daniel! How are you?

Daniel: I'm fine, thank you.

Maria: Tell me about your family.

Daniel: Sure. This is my family.

Maria: Do you have brothers and sisters?

Daniel: Yes. I have two brothers and one sister.

Maria: What is your sister's name?

Daniel: Her name is Anna.

Maria: And what is your brother's name?

Daniel: His name is Peter.

Maria: Is your father a teacher?

Daniel: Yes, he is.

Maria: And your mother?

Daniel: She is a nurse.

Maria: That's a nice family!

Daniel: Thank you!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk About Your Family

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • How big is your family?
  • Do you have brothers?
  • Do you have sisters?
  • What is your mother's name?
  • What is your father's name?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your family.

Try to use:

  • My...
  • His...
  • Her...
  • I have...
  • She has...
  • He has...
Example:

My name is David. I have a big family. I have two brothers. I have one sister. My father's name is John. My mother's name is Mary. Her name is Mary. His name is John.
