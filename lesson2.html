📚 Grammar — Personal Information

1️⃣ I am...

Use I am to give information about yourself.

I am 20 years old.
Eu tenho 20 anos.

2️⃣ I am from...

Use I am from to say your country or place of origin.

I am from Mozambique.
Eu sou de Moçambique.

3️⃣ I live in...

Use I live in to say where you live.

I live in Maputo.
Eu moro em Maputo.

4️⃣ What is your nationality?

Use this question to ask someone's nationality.

What is your nationality?
Qual é a tua nacionalidade?

I am Mozambican.
Eu sou moçambicano / moçambicana.

5️⃣ How old are you?

Use this question to ask someone's age.

How old are you?
Quantos anos tens?

I am eighteen years old.
Eu tenho dezoito anos.

6️⃣ Where do you live?

Use this question to ask where someone lives.

Where do you live?
Onde moras?

I live in Maputo.
Eu moro em Maputo.

💬 Real-life Conversation

José and Maria are meeting and sharing personal information.

José: Hello! What's your name?

Maria: Hi! My name is Maria. What's your name?

José: My name is José. Nice to meet you.

Maria: Nice to meet you too.

José: Where are you from?

Maria: I am from Mozambique.

José: Where do you live?

Maria: I live in Maputo.

José: How old are you?

Maria: I am eighteen years old. How old are you?

José: I am twenty years old.

Maria: What is your nationality?

José: I am Mozambican.

Maria: That's great!

José: It was nice talking to you.

Maria: Nice talking to you too. See you later!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk About Yourself

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • What is your name?
  • How old are you?
  • Where are you from?
  • What is your nationality?
  • Where do you live?
  • Are you a student?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about yourself.

Try to include:

  • Your name
  • Your age
  • Your country
  • Your nationality
  • Your city
  • Your occupation or school
Example:

Hello. My name is José. I am twenty years old. I am from Mozambique. I am Mozambican. I live in Maputo. I am a student. Nice to meet you.
