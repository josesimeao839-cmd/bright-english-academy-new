📚 Grammar — Using Numbers

1️⃣ Talking About Age

Use: I am + number + years old.

I am twenty years old.
Eu tenho vinte anos.

2️⃣ Asking About Age

How old are you?
Quantos anos tens?

I am eighteen years old.
Eu tenho dezoito anos.

3️⃣ Talking About Quantity

Use numbers to say how many things or people there are.

I have two brothers.
Eu tenho dois irmãos.

There are five students.
Há cinco estudantes.

4️⃣ Talking About Prices

How much is it?
Quanto custa?

It is ten dollars.
Custa dez dólares.

5️⃣ Phone Numbers

Phone numbers are usually said one number at a time.

My phone number is 82 123 4567.
O meu número de telefone é 82 123 4567.

💬 Real-life Conversation

José and Maria are talking about numbers and personal information.

José: Hi, Maria! How old are you?

Maria: I am eighteen years old. How old are you?

José: I am twenty years old.

Maria: How many brothers do you have?

José: I have two brothers.

Maria: And how many sisters do you have?

José: I have one sister.

Maria: That's nice! How many students are in your class?

José: There are thirty students.

Maria: Wow! That's a big class.

José: Yes, it is!

Maria: How much is this book?

José: It is ten dollars.

Maria: Okay. Thank you!

José: You're welcome!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk About Numbers

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • How old are you?
  • How many brothers do you have?
  • How many sisters do you have?
  • How many students are in your class?
  • What is your phone number?
  • How much is a book?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences using numbers.

Try to include:

  • Your age
  • Number of brothers
  • Number of sisters
  • Number of students in your class
  • A phone number
  • A price
Example:

I am twenty years old. I have two brothers. I have one sister. There are thirty students in my class. My phone number is 82 123 4567. My English book is ten dollars.
