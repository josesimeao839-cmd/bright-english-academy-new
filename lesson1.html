📚 Grammar — Introducing Yourself

1️⃣ My name is...

Use My name is to tell someone your name.

My name is José.
O meu nome é José.

2️⃣ I am...

Use I am to give basic information about yourself.

I am a student.
Eu sou estudante.

3️⃣ What's your name?

Use this question to ask someone's name.

What's your name?
Qual é o teu nome?

4️⃣ How are you?

Use this question to ask about someone's condition.

How are you?
Como estás?

5️⃣ Short Answers

I am fine, thank you.
Estou bem, obrigado.

I'm good.
Estou bem.

I'm great!
Estou ótimo!

💬 Real-life Conversation

José and Álvaro are meeting for the first time.

José: Hello! Good morning.

Álvaro: Good morning!

José: What's your name?

Álvaro: My name is Álvaro. What's your name?

José: My name is José. Nice to meet you.

Álvaro: Nice to meet you too.

José: How are you?

Álvaro: I'm fine, thank you. How are you?

José: I'm great, thank you!

Álvaro: See you later!

José: See you!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Introduce Yourself

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to say:

  • Hello.
  • My name is...
  • I am a student.
  • I am from...
  • Nice to meet you.
  • Thank you.

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 5–6 sentences introducing yourself.

Try to include:

  • Your name
  • Your age
  • Your country
  • Your city
  • How you are
Example:

Hello. My name is José. I am a student. I am from Mozambique. I am fine, thank you. Nice to meet you.
