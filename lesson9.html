📚 Grammar — Possessive Adjectives

Possessive adjectives show who something belongs to.

1️⃣ My

Use my when something belongs to me.

This is my mother.
Esta é a minha mãe.

My brother is a student.
O meu irmão é estudante.

2️⃣ Your

Use your when something belongs to you.

This is your sister.
Esta é a tua irmã.

What is your mother's name?
Qual é o nome da tua mãe?

3️⃣ His

Use his for something belonging to a male person.

This is his father.
Este é o pai dele.

His brother is a doctor.
O irmão dele é médico.

4️⃣ Her

Use her for something belonging to a female person.

This is her mother.
Esta é a mãe dela.

Her sister is a student.
A irmã dela é estudante.

5️⃣ Our

Use our for something belonging to us.

This is our house.
Esta é a nossa casa.

6️⃣ Their

Use their for something belonging to them.

This is their family.
Esta é a família deles.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Maria and Daniel are talking about their families.

Maria: Hi, Daniel! Tell me about your family.

Daniel: Sure. I have a small family.

Maria: How many people are in your family?

Daniel: There are four people in my family.

Maria: Who are they?

Daniel: My father, my mother, my sister and me.

Maria: What is your father's name?

Daniel: His name is John.

Maria: And what is your mother's name?

Daniel: Her name is Anna.

Maria: Do you have a sister?

Daniel: Yes. Her name is Lucy.

Maria: How old is she?

Daniel: She is twelve years old.

Maria: That's nice! I have two brothers.

Daniel: Really? What are their names?

Maria: Their names are Peter and James.

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk About Your Family

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • How many people are in your family?
  • Who are they?
  • What is your father's name?
  • What is your mother's name?
  • Do you have brothers or sisters?
  • How old are they?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your family.

Try to use:

  • My...
  • His...
  • Her...
  • Our...
  • Their...
Example:

This is my family. My father is a teacher. My mother is a nurse. I have one brother. His name is John. I have one sister. Her name is Anna. We live in our house together.
