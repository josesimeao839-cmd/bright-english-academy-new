📖 3. Grammar Review — Verb To Be

I am a student.

You are my friend.

He is a teacher.

She is a student.

We are classmates.

They are students.

Remember:

I → am

You / We / They → are

He / She / It → is

👉 6. This, That, These & Those

Word Use Example
This Singular + near This is my book.
That Singular + far That is the board.
These Plural + near These are my pens.
Those Plural + far Those are chairs.

⏰ 7. Grammar Review — Present Simple

We use the Present Simple for routines, habits and regular activities.

I study English.

You study English.

We study English.

They study English.


He studies English.

She studies English.

⚠️ Remember the third person:

He works.

She studies.

He goes to school.

🗣️ 11. Review Conversation

A: Hello! What's your name?

B: My name is Daniel. What's your name?

A: I'm Sarah. Nice to meet you.

B: Nice to meet you too.

A: Are you a student?

B: Yes, I am.

A: What time do you go to school?

B: I go to school at seven thirty.

A: What do you study?

B: I study English and mathematics.


🗣️ 12. Speaking Challenge

Introduce yourself without reading a model.

Try to talk about:

  • Your name
  • Your age
  • Your school
  • Your family
  • Your favorite color
  • Your daily routine

Challenge: Speak for at least one minute.

✍️ 13. Writing Challenge

Write a short paragraph about yourself.

Try to include at least eight sentences.

Example:

My name is Daniel. I am a student. I study English. I have a small family. My favorite color is blue. I go to school every morning. I study English every day. I go home in the afternoon.
