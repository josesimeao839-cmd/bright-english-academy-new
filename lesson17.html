📍 Prepositions of Place

Use these words to describe where a place is.

NEXT TO = ao lado de
OPPOSITE = em frente de
BETWEEN = entre
NEAR = perto de
BEHIND = atrás de
The bank is next to the supermarket.
O banco fica ao lado do supermercado.

The pharmacy is opposite the hospital.
A farmácia fica em frente ao hospital.

The park is near the school.
O parque fica perto da escola.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Ácima is asking Yasmine for directions.

Ácima: Excuse me, Yasmine. Can you help me?
Yasmine: Of course! What do you need?
Ácima: Where is the bank?
Yasmine: The bank is near the supermarket.
Ácima: How can I get there?
Yasmine: Go straight for two blocks.
Ácima: Then what?
Yasmine: Turn right at the hospital.
Ácima: Is the bank on my right?
Yasmine: Yes, it is. It is next to the supermarket.
Ácima: Thank you very much!
Yasmine: You're welcome!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk about your town.

Speak for 30–60 seconds.


Try to answer:

  • What places are in your town?
  • Where is the school?
  • Where is the supermarket?
  • Is there a hospital near your home?
  • How can someone get to the bank?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your town.

Try to use:

  • There is...
  • There are...
  • near
  • next to
  • opposite
  • go straight
  • turn left / turn right

Example:

My town is busy. There is a supermarket near my house. There is a school next to the park. There is a hospital in the town center. The bank is opposite the supermarket. There are many shops on the main street.
