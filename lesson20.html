📚 Grammar — There is / There are

1️⃣ There is

Use There is with one person or one thing.

There is + singular noun.
There is a sofa in the living room.

Há um sofá na sala.

2️⃣ There are

Use There are with two or more things.

There are + plural noun.
There are two chairs in the kitchen.

Há duas cadeiras na cozinha.

3️⃣ Negative Form

There isn't + singular noun.

There aren't + plural noun.
There isn't a television in the bedroom.
There aren't any chairs in the bathroom.

4️⃣ Questions

Is there + singular noun?

Are there + plural noun?
Is there a sofa in the living room?
Yes, there is.

Are there two bedrooms?
Yes, there are.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Juan and Laima are talking about Juan's home.

Juan: Hi, Laima! Welcome to my home.
Laima: Thank you, Juan! Your home is beautiful.
Juan: Thanks! There is a living room over there.
Laima: Is there a sofa in the living room?
Juan: Yes, there is. There is also a television.
Laima: Nice! How many bedrooms are there?
Juan: There are three bedrooms.
Laima: Are there two bathrooms?
Juan: No, there aren't. There is one bathroom.
Laima: Where is the kitchen?
Juan: The kitchen is next to the dining room.
Laima: Is there a table in the dining room?
Juan: Yes, there is a big table and six chairs.
Laima: Wow! Your home has everything!
Juan: Thank you! I'm happy you like it.
Laima: I really like it!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences describing your home.

Try to use:

  • There is...
  • There are...
  • There isn't...
  • There aren't...
  • Is there...?
  • Are there...?

Example:

There is a living room in my home. There is a kitchen next to it. There are two bedrooms. There is a big bed in my bedroom. There are two windows. There isn't a television in my bedroom. There are chairs in the kitchen.
