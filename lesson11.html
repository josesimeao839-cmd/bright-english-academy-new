📚 Present Simple — Affirmative

We use the Present Simple to talk about routines, habits and facts.

Basic structure:
I / You / We / They + base verb
He / She / It + verb + S
I study English every day.
Eu estudo inglês todos os dias.
They go to school at seven.
Eles vão à escola às sete.
She studies English every day.
Ela estuda inglês todos os dias.
He goes to work at eight.
Ele vai ao trabalho às oito.
Remember:

With he, she, it, the verb normally takes -s.

play → plays

work → works

study → studies

go → goes

❌ Negative — Don't / Doesn't

Use don't and doesn't to make negative Present Simple sentences.

I / You / We / They + don't + base verb
He / She / It + doesn't + base verb
I don't drink coffee.
Eu não bebo café.
They don't go to school on Sunday.
Eles não vão à escola no domingo.
She doesn't work on Sunday.
Ela não trabalha no domingo.
Important!

After doesn't, use the base verb.

❌ She doesn't works.

✅ She doesn't work.

❓ Questions — Do / Does

Use Do or Does to ask Present Simple questions.

Do + I / you / we / they + base verb?
Does + he / she / it + base verb?
Do you study English?
Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Does she work here?
Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Do they go to school every day?
Yes, they do.
Remember:

❌ Does she studies English?

✅ Does she study English?

🧠 Adverbs of Frequency

These words tell us how often something happens.

Always 100%
Usually 80%
Often 60%
Sometimes 40%
Rarely 20%
Never 0%
I always wake up early.
Eu acordo sempre cedo.
She usually has breakfast at seven.
Ela normalmente toma o pequeno-almoço às sete.
They sometimes play football.
Eles às vezes jogam futebol.
He never drinks coffee.
Ele nunca bebe café.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Read the conversation and practice saying it aloud.

Anna: What time do you usually wake up?
David: I usually wake up at six o'clock.
Anna: Do you have breakfast at home?
David: Yes, I do. I have breakfast at seven.
Anna: Do you go to school every day?
David: Yes, I do.
Anna: What do you do in the evening?
David: I usually do my homework and study English.
Anna: What time do you go to bed?
David: I go to bed at ten o'clock.
