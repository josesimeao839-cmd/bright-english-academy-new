📚 Grammar — Talking About Days & Months

1️⃣ What day is it?

Use this question to ask about the day.

What day is it?
Que dia é hoje?

It is Monday.
É segunda-feira.

2️⃣ What month is it?

What month is it?
Que mês é?

It is August.
É agosto.

3️⃣ Today, Tomorrow and Yesterday

Today is Monday.
Hoje é segunda-feira.

Tomorrow is Tuesday.
Amanhã é terça-feira.

Yesterday was Sunday.
Ontem foi domingo.

4️⃣ Talking About Birthdays

My birthday is in May.
O meu aniversário é em maio.

My birthday is on Monday.
O meu aniversário é na segunda-feira.

5️⃣ Asking When Something Happens

When is your English class?
Quando é a tua aula de inglês?

It is on Friday.
É na sexta-feira.

💬 Real-life Conversation

José and Maria are talking about days, months and birthdays.

José: Hi, Maria! What day is it today?

Maria: Today is Monday.

José: Oh, really? What month is it?

Maria: It is August.

José: When is your birthday?

Maria: My birthday is in December.

José: That's nice! What day is your birthday?

Maria: It is on Friday this year.

José: Great! Are you having a party?

Maria: Yes, I am.

José: What day is your English class?

Maria: My English class is on Wednesday.

José: Excellent! See you on Wednesday.

Maria: See you!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk About Your Week

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • What day is it today?
  • What day is tomorrow?
  • What day was yesterday?
  • What is your favorite day?
  • When is your birthday?
  • When is your English class?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your week and important dates.

Try to use:

  • Today is...
  • Tomorrow is...
  • Yesterday was...
  • My favorite day is...
  • My birthday is in...
  • My English class is on...
Example:

Today is Monday. Tomorrow is Tuesday. Yesterday was Sunday. My favorite day is Saturday. My birthday is in December. My English class is on Wednesday. I like Friday too.
