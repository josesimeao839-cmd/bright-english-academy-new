📚 Grammar — Asking About Prices

❓ How much is it?

Use this question to ask about the price of one thing.

How much is it?
How much is it?
It's five dollars.

👕 How much is + singular item?

How much is this shirt?
How much is this shirt?
It's twenty dollars.

👟 How much are + plural items?

How much are these shoes?
How much are these shoes?
They are thirty dollars.

🛍️ I would like...

Use this expression when you want to buy something politely.

I would like + item.
I would like this shirt, please.
Eu gostaria desta camisa, por favor.

💳 Paying

Can I pay by card?
Yes, you can.
Sim, pode.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Daniel and Sofia are shopping for clothes.

Daniel: Hi, Sofia. What are you looking for?
Sofia: Hi, Daniel. I'm looking for a new shirt.
Daniel: What color do you want?
Sofia: I want a blue shirt.
Daniel: Look! There is a blue shirt over there.
Sofia: Oh, I like it. How much is it?
Daniel: It's fifteen dollars.
Sofia: That's good. Is it cheap?
Daniel: Yes, I think it's a good price.
Sofia: Great. I would like this one, please.
Daniel: Do you want to pay by card?
Sofia: Yes. Can I pay by card?
Daniel: Yes, you can.
Sofia: Thank you!
Daniel: You're welcome!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about a shopping trip.

Try to use:

  • I want to buy...
  • How much is...?
  • It's...
  • I would like...
  • Can I pay by card?

I want to buy a new shirt. I want a blue shirt. I find one in a shop. I ask, "How much is it?" The shirt is fifteen dollars. I think it is a good price. I would like this one, please.
