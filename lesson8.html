🌅 My Daily Routine

Here is an example of a typical daily routine.

🌅 Morning

I wake up at 6:00.
Eu acordo às 6:00.

I brush my teeth.
Eu escovo os dentes.

I take a shower.
Eu tomo banho.

I have breakfast.
Eu tomo o pequeno-almoço.

☀️ Afternoon

I have lunch at 1:00.
Eu almoço à 1:00.

I study English.
Eu estudo inglês.

🌙 Evening

I go home at 5:00.
Eu vou para casa às 5:00.

I have dinner with my family.
Eu janto com a minha família.

I go to bed at 10:00.
Eu vou para a cama às 10:00.

📚 Grammar — Present Simple

1️⃣ I / You / We / They

Use the base verb with I, you, we and they.

I wake up at six.
Eu acordo às seis.

They go to school.
Eles vão à escola.

2️⃣ He / She / It

With he, she and it, normally add -s to the verb.

He wakes up at six.
Ele acorda às seis.

She goes to school.
Ela vai à escola.

3️⃣ Negative Form

Use don't with I, you, we and they.

Use doesn't with he, she and it.

I don't wake up late.
Eu não acordo tarde.

She doesn't go to school on Sunday.
Ela não vai à escola no domingo.

4️⃣ Questions

Do you wake up early?
Você acorda cedo?

Does he go to work?
Ele vai ao trabalho?

💬 Real-life Conversation

Anna and David are talking about their daily routines.

Anna: What time do you wake up?

David: I wake up at six o'clock.

Anna: What do you do after you wake up?

David: I brush my teeth and take a shower.

Anna: Do you have breakfast at home?

David: Yes, I do.

Anna: What time do you go to school?

David: I go to school at seven thirty.

Anna: What do you do in the afternoon?

David: I study English and do my homework.

Anna: What time do you go home?

David: I go home at five.

Anna: And what time do you go to bed?

David: I go to bed at ten.

Anna: That's a busy day!

David: Yes, it is!

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Describe Your Daily Routine

Speak for 30–60 seconds.

Try to answer:

  • What time do you wake up?
  • What do you do in the morning?
  • What time do you go to school or work?
  • What do you do in the afternoon?
  • What time do you go home?
  • What time do you go to bed?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your daily routine.

Try to use:

  • I wake up...
  • I have breakfast...
  • I go to...
  • I study...
  • I have lunch...
  • I go home...
  • I go to bed...
Example:

I wake up at six o'clock. I brush my teeth. I take a shower. I have breakfast at home. I go to school at seven. I study English in the afternoon. I go home at five. I go to bed at ten.
