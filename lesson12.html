❤️ Like, Love & Hate

Use these verbs to talk about things you enjoy or dislike.

I / You / We / They + like + activity
He / She / It + likes + activity
I like music.
Eu gosto de música.
She likes reading.
Ela gosta de ler.
They love football.
Eles adoram futebol.
He hates cooking.
Ele odeia cozinhar.
Negative:
I don't like...
She doesn't like...

🧠 Frequency in Real Life

Combine Present Simple with frequency expressions.

I always listen to music.
Eu sempre ouço música.
I usually play football on Saturday.
Normalmente jogo futebol no sábado.
I often watch movies.
Eu vejo filmes frequentemente.
I sometimes go shopping.
Às vezes faço compras.
I never play basketball.
Eu nunca jogo basquetebol.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Read, listen and repeat.

Maria: What do you do in your free time?
John: I usually listen to music and watch movies.
Maria: Do you play any sports?
John: Yes, I do. I play football.
Maria: How often do you play football?
John: I usually play on Saturday.
Maria: Do you like reading?
John: Yes, I do. I love reading books.
Maria: That's great!

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your free-time activities.

Use:

  • like / love / hate
  • Present Simple
  • at least two frequency expressions
  • at least three free-time activities

Example:

I usually listen to music in my free time. I like playing football. I often watch movies. I sometimes read books.
