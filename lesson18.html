📚 Grammar — There is / There are

Use There is for one thing and There are for two or more things.

Singular

There is + one thing
There is a sofa in the living room.
Há um sofá na sala.

Plural

There are + two or more things
There are two chairs in the kitchen.
Há duas cadeiras na cozinha.

Negative

There isn't + singular
There aren't + plural
There isn't a television in my bedroom.
Não há televisão no meu quarto.
There aren't any chairs in the garden.
Não há cadeiras no jardim.

Questions

Is there + singular...?
Are there + plural...?
Is there a bathroom upstairs?

Yes, there is.
No, there isn't.
Are there two bedrooms?

Yes, there are.
No, there aren't.

💬 Real-life Conversation

José is showing Álvaro his home.

José: Welcome to my house, Álvaro!
Álvaro: Thank you, José! Your house is beautiful.
José: Thanks! This is the living room.
Álvaro: Is there a television in the living room?
José: Yes, there is. There is also a big sofa.
Álvaro: Nice! How many bedrooms are there?
José: There are three bedrooms.
Álvaro: And where is the kitchen?
José: The kitchen is next to the living room.
Álvaro: Is there a garden?
José: Yes, there is a small garden behind the house.
Álvaro: Your home sounds great!
José: Thank you! I really like it.
