📚 Grammar — What are you wearing?

1️⃣ Present Continuous

We use the Present Continuous to talk about what someone is wearing now.

Subject + am / is / are + wearing + clothes
I am wearing a blue T-shirt.
Eu estou a usar uma camiseta azul.

2️⃣ He / She is wearing

He is wearing...
She is wearing...
She is wearing a red dress.
Ela está a usar um vestido vermelho.

3️⃣ Questions

What are you wearing?

What is she wearing?

What is he wearing?
What are you wearing?
I am wearing jeans and a white T-shirt.

4️⃣ Negative

I am not wearing...
She is not wearing...
They are not wearing...
She isn't wearing a jacket.
Ela não está a usar um casaco.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Felizarda and Promisse are getting ready for class.

Felizarda: Hi, Promisse! You look nice today.
Promisse: Thank you, Felizarda!
Felizarda: What are you wearing today?
Promisse: I am wearing a blue T-shirt and black jeans.
Felizarda: Nice! Are you wearing white shoes?
Promisse: Yes, I am. I really like these shoes.
Felizarda: They look great! What about your jacket?
Promisse: I am not wearing a jacket today.
Felizarda: What are you wearing, Felizarda?
Felizarda: I am wearing a green dress and black shoes.
Promisse: Your dress is beautiful!
Felizarda: Thank you! Green is my favorite color.
Promisse: I like blue. It is my favorite color.
Felizarda: We have to go to class now.
Promisse: Yes! Let's go.

🗣️ Speaking Challenge

🎤 Talk about what you are wearing.

Speak for 30–60 seconds.


Try to answer:

  • What are you wearing?
  • What color is your T-shirt or shirt?
  • What color are your shoes?
  • Are you wearing jeans?
  • Are you wearing a jacket?

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences about your clothes.

Try to use:

  • I am wearing...
  • My ... is...
  • My shoes are...
  • I am not wearing...

Example:

Today I am wearing a white T-shirt. I am wearing black jeans. My shoes are blue. I am not wearing a jacket. My clothes are comfortable. I like my outfit.
