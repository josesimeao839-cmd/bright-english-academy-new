📚 There is — One Thing

Use There is when we talk about one person or one thing.

There is + singular noun
There is a sofa in the living room.
Há um sofá na sala.
There is a bed in the bedroom.
Há uma cama no quarto.
There is a table in the kitchen.
Há uma mesa na cozinha.

📚 There are — Two or More Things

Use There are when we talk about two or more things.

There are + plural noun
There are two chairs in the kitchen.
Há duas cadeiras na cozinha.
There are three bedrooms in the house.
Há três quartos na casa.
There are many books on the table.
Há muitos livros sobre a mesa.

❌ Negative Form

Use these forms when something does not exist or is not present.

There isn't + singular noun
There aren't + plural noun
There isn't a TV in the bedroom.
Não há uma televisão no quarto.
There aren't any chairs in the garden.
Não há cadeiras no jardim.

❓ Questions: Is there? / Are there?

Is there + a/an + singular noun?
Are there + plural noun?
Is there a bathroom?
Yes, there is.
No, there isn't.
Are there two bedrooms?
Yes, there are.
No, there aren't.

📍 Prepositions of Place

Use these words to describe where things are.

English Portuguese Example
in em / dentro de The sofa is in the living room.
on em / sobre The book is on the table.
under debaixo de The bag is under the table.
next to ao lado de The chair is next to the table.
behind atrás de The garden is behind the house.

💬 Real-life Conversation

Read, listen and repeat.

Anna: Welcome to my house!
David: Thank you! Your house is beautiful.
Anna: There is a living room downstairs.
David: Is there a kitchen?
Anna: Yes, there is. It is next to the living room.
David: Are there any bedrooms upstairs?
Anna: Yes, there are two bedrooms.
David: Is there a garden?
Anna: Yes, there is a small garden behind the house.

✍️ Writing Challenge

Write 6–8 sentences describing your home.

Try to use:

  • There is
  • There are
  • There isn't
  • There aren't
  • at least two prepositions of place

Example:

There are three bedrooms in my house. There is a living room. There is a sofa in the living room. There is a kitchen next to the living room. There are two chairs around the table. There is a garden behind the house.
